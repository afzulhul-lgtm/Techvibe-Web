2026 Maserati Quattroporte Review: 430 HP Luxury Sedan Specs & Price

By Sarah Mitchell Senior Editor
Published: May 21, 2026 Updated: May 21, 2026 6 Min Read

The luxury sedan market is at a strange intersection in 2026. While German automakers are aggressively pushing heavy battery-electric vehicles, Maserati has recognized that a core group of buyers still demands the visceral thrill of internal combustion. Enter the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte. Specifically, the 430-horsepower variant serves as a critical bridge—offering premium Italian craftsmanship paired with a twin-turbo V6 that proves soul and performance haven't been entirely sacrificed to silent EV platforms.

This iteration of the Quattroporte isn't just a minor facelift. Riding on the highly adaptable STLA Large architecture, the new sedan drops the outdated Chrysler-sourced switchgear that plagued older generations. Instead, it delivers a driver-focused, tech-forward experience designed to go toe-to-toe with the Porsche Panamera and the BMW 7 Series.

Redesigned Architecture and 430-HP Powertrain

Maserati officially retired its Ferrari-derived V8 engines recently, a move that worried purists. However, the 2026 Quattroporte silences those concerns with a specifically tuned iteration of the 3.0-liter twin-turbocharged Nettuno V6. Pushing 430 horsepower and roughly 400 lb-ft of torque, this engine provides a raspy, aggressive exhaust note that you simply cannot get from an Audi or a Mercedes in this price bracket.

Power is routed through an eight-speed ZF automatic transmission, which remains one of the sharpest torque-converter gearboxes in the industry. Combined with a rear-biased Q4 all-wheel-drive system, the 430 HP Quattroporte hauls its luxury bulk from 0 to 60 mph in an estimated 4.5 seconds. The suspension tuning leans slightly firmer than an S-Class, prioritizing cornering capability and steering feedback over cloud-like isolation.

Interior Upgrades: Sonus Faber and Tech Integration

Inside, the 2026 Quattroporte is completely unrecognizable from its predecessor. Maserati has finally delivered an interior that justifies a six-figure price tag. You won't find generic plastic buttons here. The cabin is draped in premium Poltrona Frau leather, genuine open-pore wood, and cold-touch aluminum trim.

The technological leap is equally massive. The dashboard is anchored by a sharp 12.3-inch central touchscreen running the Android Automotive-based Maserati Intelligent Assistant (MIA). It responds instantly and features wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. For audiophiles, the standard audio setup is impressive, but the optional 21-speaker Sonus faber 3D sound system is a masterpiece, turning the cabin into a rolling concert hall.

Pricing Strategy and Market Competitors

Dealerships are preparing for the 430-HP base model to carry a starting price of approximately $115,000 before destination fees. Once you start adding premium paint options, the upgraded Sonus faber audio, and advanced driver-assistance packages, buyers should expect out-the-door pricing closer to the $135,000 mark.

This pricing strategy places the Quattroporte directly against the Porsche Panamera 4 and the BMW 740i. While the BMW offers superior rear-seat luxury and the Panamera delivers slightly sharper track dynamics, the Maserati offers something less tangible: exclusivity and character. You buy a 7 Series to be driven in; you buy a Quattroporte because you want to drive.

Key Takeaways

  • The 2026 Quattroporte utilizes a 3.0L twin-turbo Nettuno V6 pushing 430 horsepower.
  • It features a massive technology overhaul, highlighted by a 12.3-inch Android Automotive screen and an available 21-speaker Sonus faber sound system.
  • With a starting price of $115,000, it targets buyers looking for a driver-centric alternative to the BMW 7 Series and Mercedes S-Class.

Is It Worth the Premium?

If your sole metric for buying a luxury car is standard feature count per dollar, the German competitors might win on a spreadsheet. The Mercedes-Benz S-Class remains the undisputed king of sheer technological isolation, and Audi continues to offer brilliant digital interfaces.

However, the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte isn't built for spreadsheets. It is built for buyers who find modern luxury cars too sterile. The tactile feel of the steering, the sharp bark of the exhaust on a cold start, and the striking exterior lines offer an emotional connection that is becoming rare in this segment. It is an imperfect, brilliant piece of Italian engineering that commands attention in every parking lot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the starting price of the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte?

The 2026 Maserati Quattroporte 430 HP variant is expected to have a starting base price of approximately $115,000 before destination charges and optional upgrades.

What engine produces the 430 HP in the 2026 model?

The 430 horsepower is generated by a specifically tuned version of Maserati's 3.0-liter twin-turbocharged Nettuno V6 engine, paired with an eight-speed ZF automatic transmission.

How fast is the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte 430 HP?

Equipped with the rear-biased Q4 all-wheel-drive system, the 430 HP Quattroporte is estimated to accelerate from 0 to 60 mph in roughly 4.5 seconds.

Who are the primary competitors for the 2026 Quattroporte?

The primary competitors in the full-size luxury sedan segment include the Porsche Panamera 4, BMW 740i, Audi A8, and the Mercedes-Benz S-Class.
