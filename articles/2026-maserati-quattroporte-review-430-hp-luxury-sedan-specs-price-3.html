2026 Maserati Quattroporte Review: 430 HP Luxury Sedan Specs & Price

By Sarah Mitchell Senior Editor
Published: May 21, 2026 Updated: May 21, 2026 6 Min Read

Releasing in Q3 2026, the Maserati Quattroporte retains its internal combustion roots in a segment rapidly shifting toward battery-electric platforms. The core 430-horsepower variant utilizes a precision-tuned twin-turbo V6 to bridge the gap between traditional Italian performance and modern emissions requirements, providing a distinct alternative to the silent drivetrains of its German rivals.

Transitioning to the STLA Large platform, the 2026 chassis extends the wheelbase by 1.2 inches to increase rear legroom while maintaining a 50:50 weight distribution. Maserati officially discarded the legacy Chrysler-sourced interior components, replacing them with bespoke switchgear and an entirely new digital interface designed to compete directly against the Porsche Panamera 4 and BMW 740i.

Engine Specifications: 430-HP Nettuno V6

Displacing 3.0 liters, the twin-turbocharged Nettuno V6 engine generates 430 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque in the base configuration. This specific calibration features pre-chamber combustion technology derived from Formula 1, which enhances thermal efficiency and sharpens throttle response compared to the outgoing Ferrari-built V8 blocks.

Routing power through a ZF 8-speed automatic transmission, the rear-biased Q4 all-wheel-drive system propels the 4,400-pound sedan from 0 to 60 mph in 4.5 seconds. Top speed is electronically limited to 175 mph. While these acceleration metrics trail the lighter Porsche Panamera 4 by 0.2 seconds, the Quattroporte prioritizes mid-range passing power and a specifically engineered exhaust resonance that bypasses synthetic audio enhancement.

Interior Upgrades: STLA Platform and Sonus Faber

Measuring 12.3 inches, the central infotainment display runs the new Android Automotive-powered Maserati Intelligent Assistant (MIA). System latency has been reduced by 40% compared to previous generations, allowing instantaneous access to wireless Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and integrated climate controls.

Costing an additional $4,500 over the base Harman Kardon setup, the optional 21-speaker Sonus faber 3D audio system pushes 1,285 watts of amplification through laser-cut metal grilles. Material quality sees a significant upgrade, wrapping the cabin in Poltrona Frau leather and open-pore wood trim, though the capacitive touch controls on the steering wheel occasionally register accidental inputs during aggressive cornering.

Pricing, Options, and Market Competitors

Starting at $115,000 before a standard $1,495 destination fee, the 2026 Quattroporte commands a premium over its direct rivals. Fully optioned models equipped with advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and premium paint finishes frequently exceed $135,000 on dealership floors.

Positioned against the BMW 740i and Mercedes-Benz S-Class, the Maserati sacrifices rear-cabin isolation and autonomous driving capabilities in favor of tactile steering feedback and a firmer suspension calibration. Buyers seeking a chauffeur-driven experience will find the German options superior, but the Quattroporte secures an advantage for owners who prefer the driver's seat.

Key Takeaways

  • $115,000 base MSRP positions the 430-HP Quattroporte as a premium driver-focused option in the full-size sedan segment.
  • 3.0-liter twin-turbo Nettuno V6 delivers 430 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque, utilizing pre-chamber combustion.
  • Interior refinements feature a 12.3-inch Android Automotive display and Poltrona Frau leather, replacing legacy Chrysler components.
  • Q4 all-wheel-drive system enables a 4.5-second 0-60 mph acceleration time, prioritizing mechanical grip over automated driving features.

Actionable Conclusion

Spending $115,000 on the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte requires prioritizing mechanical character over maximum technological features. Buyers requiring class-leading rear legroom or Level 3 autonomous driving should secure an allocation for a BMW 7 Series. Conversely, driving enthusiasts seeking a highly responsive V6 powertrain, Italian exterior styling, and engaging chassis dynamics should test drive the Quattroporte before the industry fully transitions to electric powertrains.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the starting price of the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte?

Hitting dealership showrooms with an estimated base price of $115,000, the 2026 Quattroporte requires an additional $1,495 destination charge. Upgrading paint, wheels, and interior trims quickly pushes the final transaction price past $135,000.

What engine produces the 430 HP in the 2026 model?

Replacing the legacy V8, a 3.0-liter twin-turbocharged Nettuno V6 generates 430 horsepower. This engine utilizes F1-derived pre-chamber ignition and routes power through an eight-speed ZF automatic transmission.

How fast is the 2026 Maserati Quattroporte 430 HP?

Utilizing the Q4 all-wheel-drive system to maximize mechanical traction, the 430-horsepower sedan accelerates from 0 to 60 mph in 4.5 seconds and reaches an electronically governed top speed of 175 mph.

Who are the primary competitors for the 2026 Quattroporte?

Competing in the full-size executive luxury sedan market, the 2026 Quattroporte battles the Porsche Panamera 4, BMW 740i, Audi A8, and the Mercedes-Benz S-Class for market share among high-net-worth buyers.
