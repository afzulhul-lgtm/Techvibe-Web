Samsung launched the Galaxy S26 Ultra in February 2026 and made it available globally from March 11, 2026. It is Samsung's third-generation AI phone and arrives with two genuine firsts: the world's first built-in hardware Privacy Display on any smartphone, and the fastest charging ever on a Galaxy S Ultra at 60W wired / 25W wireless. Everything else — Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, 200MP F1.4 camera, 5,000mAh battery, and a refreshed Galaxy AI suite — is built on top of a foundation that Samsung's Newsroom described as generating "exceptional early demand with pre-orders surpassing previous records," with the Ultra selected by more than 70% of pre-order customers.

The Galaxy Buds4 Pro — Samsung's new flagship earbuds, priced at $229 — launch as the designated audio companion for the S26 Ultra, adding 24-bit Hi-Res Audio, improved ANC, and a unique Hearapy feature designed to reduce motion sickness during travel.

Design and Build: Slimmer, Lighter, New Camera Island

The S26 Ultra's design marks a clean break from the S24 Ultra's angular, industrial aesthetic. Samsung has adopted the Camera Island design language — the same unified, glass-integrated camera module seen on the S26 and S26+ — giving the Ultra a more cohesive, architectural appearance. The result is a phone that looks less like a piece of tactical equipment and more like a refined luxury object.

At 7.9mm thick and 214g, the S26 Ultra is 0.7mm thinner and 18g lighter than the S24 Ultra. That is a meaningful reduction for a device this large, and it makes extended one-handed use noticeably more comfortable. The corners are more rounded. The Armor Aluminum 2 frame replaces the titanium used on the S24 Ultra — a decision that has drawn some criticism, though the frame remains extremely durable by any reasonable standard.

Note on Frame Material

The move from titanium to Armor Aluminum 2 is the most-discussed design criticism. Samsung's rationale: aluminum enables the slimmer, lighter body while maintaining structural integrity. PhoneArena's review notes the phone "feels premium" despite the material change, and scores it 8/10 for design.

Privacy Display: The World First Feature

The single most distinctive hardware feature of the S26 Ultra is the built-in Privacy Display — not a film, not a screen protector, but a native display technology integrated into the panel itself. Samsung describes it as a world first for any mobile phone.

The Privacy Display allows users to restrict screen visibility so that only the person directly in front sees the content clearly — angles to the side appear dark and unreadable. Users can toggle privacy for the entire screen, specific apps, incoming notifications, or sensitive inputs like PINs, patterns, and passwords when accessing Settings, Lock Screen, and Secure Folder.

This is particularly valuable in public spaces: on public transport, in coffee shops, in airports — anywhere someone might glance at your screen. The practical applications are obvious, and the implementation in hardware (rather than a software dimming trick) produces a genuinely effective result according to every hands-on review published at launch.

Display: 6.9-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X

The S26 Ultra's 6.9-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display is one of the best screens on any smartphone in 2026. At 1440×3120 resolution (500ppi) with a 2600 nit peak brightness and 120Hz adaptive refresh rate (scaling down to 1Hz for static content), it delivers exceptional clarity in direct sunlight, vivid color accuracy, and buttery smooth scrolling. HDR10+ support ensures compatible streaming content looks spectacular.

Samsung has also added their new DX anti-reflective coating — a significant upgrade over the standard coating on previous models. In direct sunlight testing, PhoneArena rates it 9/10 for display quality, one of the highest scores in the category.

Camera System: 200MP F1.4 Wide + Quad Lenses

The camera system is the S26 Ultra's headline attraction, and the widened aperture on the main lens is its most significant real-world improvement.

The 200MP Wide camera now shoots at F1.4 — wider than the S25 Ultra's F1.7. This delivers a 47% improvement in light capture, translating directly to cleaner, less noisy images in low-light conditions. Samsung describes this as part of their Nightography video improvements, which keep footage vibrant even in challenging lighting.

The full quad-camera system:

Video capabilities include 8K@30fps and 4K@120fps, Super Steady mode with a new horizontal lock option for greater stability during dynamic movement, and the new Professional Video Codec (APV) for lossless editing compression — targeting content creators who need maximum post-production flexibility.

PhoneArena gives the S26 Ultra's camera 8.2/10 for photo quality and 7.1/10 for video quality — noting that the improvements are real but incremental rather than generational, with the main benefit being better low-light performance and faster autofocus.

Performance: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 + 16GB RAM

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy is a custom-tuned version of Qualcomm's flagship 3nm chip, built specifically for Samsung. Compared to the S25 Ultra's Snapdragon 8 Elite, it delivers:

MetricImprovement vs S25 Ultra
CPU performance+19% — nearly 3× the CPU gain of standard S26/S26+
GPU (Adreno 840)Improved ray tracing, smoother gaming at high settings
AI processing (NPU)Faster on-device AI for Galaxy AI features and camera processing
Thermal managementImproved Vapor Chamber reduces throttling under sustained load

All S26 Ultra models come with 16GB LPDDR5X RAM — 4GB more than the base S25 Ultra. PhoneArena gives it 8.9/10 for heavy performance and 9/10 for light performance, both above-average for its price class. Ray tracing is supported across the Galaxy S26 series.

Battery and Super Fast Charging 3.0

The S26 Ultra's 5,000mAh battery is unchanged in size from the S25 Ultra — a point of criticism from those who were hoping for a larger cell. In real-world use, Samsung rates it at 31 hours of video playback, and PhoneArena gives it 7.6/10 for battery life.

Galaxy AI Features: Photo Assist, Audio Eraser, Creative Studio

Samsung's Galaxy AI suite has been updated significantly for the S26 series. Key new and improved features on the S26 Ultra:

Photo Assist (upgraded): Now allows users to refine images by describing what they want in plain language — "remove the person in the background" or "change the sky to sunset" — using natural language prompts. The previous version required selecting tools manually.

Creative Studio: A new Galaxy AI tool that lets users generate stickers, invitations, and personalized wallpapers from text prompts. Aimed at casual creative use without requiring a third-party app.

Audio Eraser: Removes specific background noise from videos while preserving voices and key sounds. Useful for removing wind noise, crowd noise, or ambient sound from footage recorded in challenging environments.

Live Translate (enhanced): Real-time call translation expanded to more languages. Works on phone calls and video calls without requiring the other party to use a Samsung device.

Note Assist and Transcript Assist: AI summarization of voice recordings and notes, now with better handling of technical vocabulary and multi-speaker transcription.

Samsung Account Required

Most Galaxy AI features — Photo Assist, Audio Eraser, Creative Studio, and AI-powered translation — require a Samsung account login to function. Users who prefer not to link a Samsung account will have reduced access to the AI feature set.

Galaxy Buds4 Pro: 24-bit Hi-Fi Audio + Hearapy

The Galaxy Buds4 Pro ($229) launch as Samsung's flagship wireless earbuds designed to pair seamlessly with the S26 Ultra. They feature a new blade design — a significant visual departure from previous Galaxy Buds — and carry Samsung's most advanced audio and health features to date.

The Hearapy feature is the most unique addition to any wireless earbuds in 2026. Working through a dedicated Samsung app, Hearapy uses audio therapy techniques to reduce the symptoms of motion sickness — the kind that affects passengers during winding car journeys, long coach rides, or boat travel. For anyone who regularly travels and experiences motion sickness, this is a genuinely useful addition that no competitor currently offers.

The Intelligent Equalizer 2.0 adapts its audio profile based on the user's ear shape, detected through the fit test process. This means the sound signature each user hears is tuned to their specific ear canal geometry rather than a generic profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the camera specs of the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra?

Quad-camera: 200MP Wide F1.4 (47% brighter than S25 Ultra), 10MP telephoto 3x, 50MP periscope telephoto 5x, 12MP ultrawide. Shoots 8K@30fps, 4K@120fps, Super Steady with horizontal lock, and supports the new APV professional video codec.

What is the Privacy Display on the Galaxy S26 Ultra?

The world's first smartphone with a built-in hardware privacy display. Users can restrict screen visibility to the person directly in front — side-angle viewers see a dark, unreadable screen. Toggleable per-app, per-notification, or for the entire screen.

How fast is Super Fast Charging 3.0?

The S26 Ultra supports 60W wired charging (Super Fast Charging 3.0) and 25W wireless charging — upgrades from 45W wired and 15W wireless on the S25 Ultra. Battery is 5,000mAh rated at 31 hours video playback.

What chip does the Galaxy S26 Ultra use?

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy — custom 3nm chip from Qualcomm with Adreno 840 GPU. Delivers 19% CPU improvement over S25 Ultra. All models come with 16GB LPDDR5X RAM.

What are the key features of Galaxy Buds4 Pro?

24-bit/96kHz Hi-Res Audio, Intelligent Noise Cancellation 2.0, Intelligent Equalizer 2.0 (adapts to ear shape), Super Clear calls, and Hearapy for motion sickness reduction. New blade design. Priced at $229.

What is Samsung Hearapy?

A Samsung app built into Galaxy Buds4 Pro that uses audio therapy to reduce motion sickness during travel — particularly useful on winding roads or long journeys. No other wireless earbuds currently offer a comparable feature.

How much does the Galaxy S26 Ultra cost?

US prices: $1,299 (256GB), $1,419 (512GB), $1,659 (1TB). Samsung offered up to $900 off with trade-in. AT&T had a $0 promotional deal with any eligible trade-in at launch.

How does the Galaxy S26 Ultra compare to the S25 Ultra?

Key upgrades: Privacy Display, wider F1.4 camera aperture (+47% brightness), 60W charging (from 45W), 25W wireless (from 15W), 19% faster CPU, 16GB RAM, slimmer and lighter body. Trade-offs: frame moves from titanium to aluminum, battery size unchanged, still no Qi2/MagSafe.

Verdict

TechVibe Verdict — 8.5 / 10

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra is the best Galaxy S Ultra ever made in terms of raw specifications and hardware innovation. The Privacy Display is a genuine first and genuinely useful. The camera improvements are real even if incremental. The charging upgrades are the most meaningful quality-of-life improvement in the lineup's recent history. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with 16GB RAM ensures it will remain performant for years.

The criticisms — no Qi2, unchanged battery size, aluminum frame replacing titanium — are real but livable. If you're on an S23 Ultra or older, this is an easy upgrade. If you're coming from the S25 Ultra, the Privacy Display and charging speed are the only compelling reasons to switch. If you're in the Android ecosystem and want the best Android phone of 2026, the Galaxy S26 Ultra is the answer.
