iOS 27 Leak: Apple's Biggest iPhone Overhaul in 10 Years — Every New Feature Revealed Before WWDC

By Mike Reynolds Senior Tech Editor
Published: May 29, 2026 Updated: May 29, 2026 10 Min Read

Ten days before Apple's WWDC 2026 keynote on June 8, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman published the most detailed internal look at iOS 27 yet — complete with rendered illustrations based on sources inside Apple. What they show is not incremental. The new Siri is a fundamentally different product: a dedicated chatbot app powered by a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Google Gemini model, integrated directly into the Dynamic Island, and opened for the first time to third-party AI agents including ChatGPT and Claude. Alongside it comes a rebuilt Camera app, AI-powered Photos tools, and a philosophical shift that makes iOS 27 the most consequential Apple software release since iOS 7 redesigned the entire operating system back in 2013.

This article compiles every confirmed and credibly sourced iOS 27 leak as of May 29, 2026 — covering Siri, Camera, Photos, Apple Intelligence, Wallet, Shortcuts, compatibility, and the release timeline. We also cover the important context: Tim Cook's final WWDC as CEO, the $250 million class action Apple settled over delayed Siri promises, and what the Gemini deal actually means for your iPhone.

  • Siri is being fully rebuilt on Google Gemini (1.2T parameter model), with a standalone app, conversation history, Dynamic Island integration, and text/voice modes — the biggest Siri redesign since 2011
  • A new "Search or Ask" interface replaces Spotlight — swipe down from the top to trigger it system-wide
  • iOS 27 opens Siri to third-party AI agents: ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini will be available via a new Extensions system
  • The Camera app gets a dedicated Siri shooting mode and a professional widget panel with manual controls
  • New Photos AI tools — Extend, Enhance, and Reframe — arrive as standalone editing features
  • iOS 27 drops iPhone 11 and iPhone SE (2nd gen) support; Apple Intelligence still requires iPhone 15 Pro or newer
  • WWDC keynote: June 8, 2026 at 10am Pacific. Public release: September 2026 alongside iPhone 18
  • This is Tim Cook's final WWDC as CEO — he steps down September 1 in favour of hardware chief John Ternus

Why iOS 27 Matters More Than Any Recent Update

To understand why iOS 27 is different from the past five years of Apple software releases, you need to understand what the past two years cost Apple. At WWDC 2024, the company promised a "personalised Siri" that would understand your screen, your messages, your calendar, and respond to complex multi-step requests with genuine intelligence. Then in March 2025, Apple quietly delayed it indefinitely, citing quality problems. A class action lawsuit followed. By May 2026, Apple had settled for $250 million — with eligible iPhone 15 and 16 buyers able to claim up to $95 per device — an extraordinary admission that real products did not deliver what Apple's own marketing had promised.

Meanwhile, Google had rebuilt its Assistant into Gemini. OpenAI was serving over a billion users. Microsoft had woven Copilot through Windows. Apple, the company that invented the smartphone AI assistant with Siri in 2011, had fallen embarrassingly behind. iOS 27 is the company's architectural correction — not a feature refresh, but a fundamental restructuring of how Siri works, what it runs on, and how it integrates with the rest of the operating system.

Historic Context: Tim Cook's Final WWDC

WWDC 2026 will be Tim Cook's last keynote as Apple CEO. Cook steps down on September 1, 2026, handing leadership to John Ternus — the engineer who oversaw the transition to Apple Silicon. It is an extraordinary handover moment: Cook presents the software architecture at WWDC, and Ternus ships it inside the iPhone 18 and the long-rumoured iPhone Fold this autumn. iOS 27 is the operating system Cook leaves behind as his defining AI statement.

Siri 2.0 — Google Gemini, Standalone App & Dynamic Island

🤖 The New Siri: A Dedicated Chatbot App

Source: Bloomberg / Mark Gurman

For the first time in Siri's 15-year history, it will exist as a standalone app on the iPhone home screen. The app functions like a modern AI chatbot — think ChatGPT or Gemini with Apple's interface polish — and supports both text and voice input, persistent conversation history, image uploads, document analysis, and web search. Sessions can be set to auto-expire for users concerned about privacy.

Under the hood, the new Siri is powered by a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Google Gemini model that Apple licensed on January 12, 2026, in a deal reportedly worth around $1 billion per year. Apple's own Foundation Models had proved inadequate for the overhaul in internal testing — a rare and very public acknowledgement that the company could not solve this problem alone.

💧 Dynamic Island & "Search or Ask" Interface

Source: Bloomberg renders / MacRumors

When Siri is invoked in iOS 27, the Dynamic Island expands into a glowing pill-shaped animation — a far more polished activation experience than the current bottom-of-screen glow. Rich response cards pop directly out of the Dynamic Island, delivering structured information on places, people, news, and more without requiring the user to open a full app.

Separately, a new system-wide panel called "Search or Ask" replaces Spotlight as the primary search interface. It is triggered by swiping down from the top-centre of the screen. The panel displays recent searches, frequently-used apps, a weather widget, and a live Siri query bar — effectively merging web search, app launching, and AI reasoning into a single gesture that works from any screen in iOS.

Third-Party AI Extensions: ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini in Siri

Perhaps the most strategically significant change in iOS 27 is this: Apple is opening Siri to rival AI models for the first time. Through a new Extensions system, users will be able to install third-party AI agents from the App Store — and route their Siri queries directly to those agents as their default responses. ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude are the three confirmed initial partners.

This is a significant philosophical shift for a company that has traditionally treated its software stack as entirely proprietary. It acknowledges that no single AI model is best at everything, and that users should have the freedom to choose. From a strategic perspective, it also allows Apple to offer best-in-class AI responses without bearing the full model development cost itself — while keeping the user experience, the hardware, and the privacy architecture under Apple's control.

OpenAI, notably, raised concerns ahead of WWDC that its existing ChatGPT integration in Apple Intelligence was difficult for users to find. The company is reviewing a potential breach-of-contract claim. The introduction of the Extensions system — which would provide clearer, more prominent placement for all third-party AI partners — may be partly a response to those concerns.

Camera App Overhaul — Siri Mode & Professional Controls

📷 Siri as a Camera Mode

Source: Bloomberg

In iOS 27, Siri will appear as a dedicated shooting mode in the Camera app — sitting alongside Photo, Video, Portrait, and Panorama in the mode carousel. When in Siri mode, the shutter button is replaced by the Apple Intelligence logo. Users can direct the camera with natural language: ask Siri to frame a specific shot, identify subjects, suggest compositions, or trigger the shutter on a voice command.

This is also designed with Apple's future hardware in mind. Camera-equipped AirPods — reportedly in development for late 2026 — and smart glasses targeted for 2027 will feed visual data directly into Siri. The iOS 27 Camera architecture lays the software groundwork for this vision now, before the hardware ships.

⚙️ Professional Widget Panel

Source: Code discovered on Apple servers (Nicolás Alvarez)

Advanced users will gain access to an "Add Widgets" panel in the Camera app that allows them to replace the top row of camera shortcuts with professional controls: manual depth adjustments, exposure compensation, timer settings, Night mode override, and lens switching. This is a meaningful step toward the kind of manual control that photography enthusiasts have long wanted from the native Camera app, removing the need for third-party camera apps for most professional scenarios.

Photos AI: Extend, Enhance & Reframe

Three new AI editing tools are confirmed for the Photos app in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27:

  • Extend — Uses generative AI to expand the borders of a photo beyond its original frame. Point a photo at a landscape and ask Apple Intelligence to extend the sky, the foreground, or the sides. Similar in concept to Google Photos' Magic Editor, but executed natively within Apple's on-device AI pipeline.
  • Enhance — A comprehensive AI sharpening and detail recovery tool that applies intelligent upscaling and noise reduction. Particularly useful for older photos taken in low light on previous iPhone generations.
  • Reframe — Intelligently repositions the subject of a photo within the frame, filling in the background with AI-generated content where required. Allows a portrait taken in landscape orientation to be convincingly recomposed as a square or portrait crop without visible distortion.

These tools will be limited to iPhone 15 Pro and newer devices due to the on-device processing demands of the underlying AI models.

Visual Intelligence Upgrades

🔍 Scan Nutrition Labels Directly to Health App

Source: Server-side code / MacRumors

Visual Intelligence in iOS 27 will allow users to point the camera at a nutrition label on any food packaging and automatically import calories, macronutrients, sodium, sugar, and fibre data directly into the Health app — tagged to a specific meal time. This removes the current friction of manually searching for foods in the Health app or using a third-party calorie-tracking application, and is expected to be a significant driver of Health app engagement for the first time.

📇 Scan Business Cards & Numbers to Contacts

Source: Server-side code / MacRumors

Visual Intelligence will also be able to scan phone numbers and addresses printed on paper, business cards, or signs, and add them directly to the Contacts app with a single tap — automatically parsing the name, number, email, address, and company from the visual data. This feature has existed in third-party apps for years; its arrival as a native iOS feature will likely displace most dedicated business card scanner apps.

Every Other Confirmed iOS 27 Feature

Apple Wallet "Create a Pass": A new "+" button in the Wallet app allows users to manually create their own digital passes — event tickets, loyalty cards, gift vouchers — without requiring a third-party app to generate them. Currently visible in beta versions of the OS discovered by researchers ahead of WWDC.

Natural-Language Shortcuts: The Shortcuts app is being redesigned so users can describe in plain language what they want automated — "every morning at 7am, turn off Do Not Disturb and send my daily schedule to Sarah" — and have the system build the automation without requiring any workflow builder interaction. This could finally make Shortcuts genuinely accessible to non-technical users.

AI Wallpaper Generation: Users will be able to describe a wallpaper in text and have Apple Intelligence generate a custom image — natively, on-device. Image generation will also be available through the existing Image Playground app, with third-party generation models expected to be supported via the Extensions system.

System-Wide Grammar Checker: An AI-powered grammar and style checker will be available across all text input fields in iOS — not just Notes and Mail, but also third-party apps, Messages, and Safari text forms — automatically underlining grammatical issues with suggested corrections in a Writing Tools panel.

Redesigned AirPods Settings Panel: Bloomberg separately reported a redesigned AirPods settings interface with a more intuitive layout, better accessibility controls, and deeper integration with the new Siri — allowing AirPods to serve as an audio input interface to the Gemini-backed assistant without requiring the iPhone to be unlocked.

Refined Liquid Glass Design: iOS 26 introduced the Liquid Glass visual language — the translucent, dynamic material that replaced the flatter look of iOS 16. iOS 27 continues to refine it based on developer feedback, with smoother transitions, improved legibility in high-contrast modes, and better performance on older supported devices.

The requirement for iPhone 15 Pro or newer to access Apple Intelligence features — including the new Gemini-powered Siri — is tied to the A17 Pro chip's Neural Engine capacity. The standard A16 Bionic in the iPhone 15 and iPhone 14 Pro is not considered powerful enough for on-device processing at the scale the new Siri requires. Users on these devices will receive iOS 27 updates covering Liquid Glass refinements, Camera improvements, and bug fixes, but will not have access to the AI features.

macOS 27, announced alongside iOS 27 at WWDC, will be the first macOS version to drop Intel Mac support entirely — requiring Apple Silicon. Macs with Intel processors released before November 2020 will not receive macOS 27.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will iOS 27 be announced?

Apple will officially announce iOS 27 at WWDC 2026 on Monday, June 8, 2026, beginning at 10am Pacific Time. The keynote is streamed on Apple.com, the Apple TV app, and Apple's YouTube channel. Developer betas will be available immediately after the keynote.

What are the biggest iOS 27 leaked features?

The most significant confirmed leaks include: a rebuilt Siri powered by a custom 1.2T Google Gemini model; a standalone Siri chatbot app with conversation history and Dynamic Island integration; the new "Search or Ask" panel replacing Spotlight; third-party AI extensions (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini); a Siri shooting mode in the Camera app; new Photos AI tools (Extend, Enhance, Reframe); Visual Intelligence nutrition label scanning; the Apple Wallet "Create a Pass" feature; AI wallpaper generation; and natural-language Shortcuts automation.

Which iPhones will get iOS 27?

iOS 27 is expected to drop the iPhone 11 series and iPhone SE (2nd generation). The iPhone 12 and newer should receive the update. However, the new Gemini-powered Siri and Apple Intelligence features will require an iPhone 15 Pro or newer. The final list will be confirmed at WWDC on June 8.

Is iOS 27 Siri actually powered by Google Gemini?

Yes — on January 12, 2026, Apple and Google jointly announced that Apple's next Foundation Models will be built on a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model. Apple is reportedly paying approximately $1 billion per year. The new Siri will also support third-party AI extensions including ChatGPT and Claude via iOS 27's new Extensions system.

When is the iOS 27 public release date?

iOS 27 is expected to release publicly in September 2026, alongside the iPhone 18. Developer betas begin June 8; public betas follow in July. Based on Apple's historical release pattern, expect the final release around September 19, 2026.

Conclusion

iOS 27 is not a cosmetic refresh. It is Apple's acknowledgement that the AI assistant race cannot be won with internal resources alone, and its attempt to close a two-year gap in a single software cycle. The Gemini deal, the standalone Siri app, the Extensions system, the rebuilt Camera, the Photos AI tools — each of these individually would be a headline feature for most software updates. Together, they represent the kind of complete platform rethink Apple has not delivered since iOS 7 in 2013.

The risks are real. Apple is shipping a core OS feature powered by a competitor's model. The privacy implications of routing Siri queries through Google infrastructure — even a licensed, custom-built version — will be scrutinised heavily by privacy researchers and regulators. OpenAI's existing concerns about discoverability may escalate further once the Extensions system is announced. And the question of whether all of this actually works as demonstrated has hung over every Apple Intelligence announcement since 2024.

But on paper, and in the leaked renders, iOS 27 looks like the update Apple has been promising for three years. June 8 will tell us whether it is.
