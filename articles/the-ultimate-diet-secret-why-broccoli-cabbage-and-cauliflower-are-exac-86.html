Common Mistakes to Avoid

When it comes to incorporating broccoli, cabbage, and cauliflower into your diet, there are some common mistakes to avoid. One of the biggest mistakes is overcooking these vegetables, which can lead to a loss of nutrients and a less than appealing texture. On the flip side, undercooking can also be a problem, as it can lead to a lack of digestibility and a higher risk of foodborne illness.
